# The kitchen screen

Open the system and tap **Kitchen**. Leave it open all day.

## Reading the board

Three columns, oldest ticket first — always work from the top.

- **New** — paid and waiting.
- **Being made** — someone has started it.
- **Ready to hand over** — done, waiting for the customer.

Each ticket shows the order number, whether it is takeaway or eat in, the
customer's name if there is one, every item with its choices, and anything the
cashier typed: *no onion*, *extra chilli*. Read those lines; they are the
difference between a good order and a complaint.

Tickets go **amber after eight minutes** and **red after fifteen**. A red
ticket is one the customer has been waiting on too long.

## Working a ticket

- **Start** when you begin it.
- **Ready** when it is finished and plated.
- **Handed over** when the customer has it. The ticket leaves the board.

Tap in order.

**Tapped the wrong one?** For eight seconds after every tap a bar at the
bottom says what you did, with an **Undo** button. Tap it and the ticket goes
straight back. After that, the small **↶** arrow on a ticket moves it back
one step: from *Ready* to *Being made*, or from *Being made* to *New*.

## The chime

Switch **Sound** on at the top of the board and a short two-note chime plays
whenever a new ticket arrives, so nobody has to watch the screen. The screen
remembers the setting. If it stays silent, tap anywhere on the page once:
browsers only allow sound after someone has touched the page.

## The station buttons

**Kitchen**, **Cold station** and **Juice bar** narrow the board to your own
work. Tap **Everything** to see it all again.

## When something runs out

Tell whoever manages the menu to mark it sold out, and it leaves the till at
once so no one can order it again. If only one choice is finished — no avocado
today — that single choice can be marked instead, and the rest of the bowl
stays on sale.

## If the board stops moving

New orders appear by themselves, within seconds. If nothing has appeared for a
while and the till is busy, check the top of the screen:

- **Live** — the board is connected and will update itself.
- **Checking every few seconds** — the live connection dropped, and it is
  asking for new orders instead. It still works, just a little slower.

If neither appears, refresh the page.
