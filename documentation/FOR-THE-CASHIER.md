# The till — for the cashier

Open the system at the address written on the card by the till, and sign in
with your own email and password. Never use someone else's: every sale is
recorded under whoever is signed in.

## Starting your shift

1. Count the float in the drawer.
2. Open **Till**. It asks for the opening float — type what you counted.
3. Tap **Open the till**.

Until you do this, the system will not take an order.

## Taking an order

1. Tap the items. A bowl or burrito opens the choices — base, protein,
   toppings, sauce, size. The price at the bottom changes as you tap.
   **Add to the order** stays grey until the required choices are made.
2. Change quantities with **−** and **+**. Type anything special in the small
   box: *no onion*, *extra chilli*.
3. Choose **Takeaway**, **Eat in** or **Delivery**, and take the customer's
   name if it is a big order.
4. **Ask for their phone number** and type it in **Phone (stamp card)**. Their
   card appears: every paid bowl or burrito earns a stamp, and a full card
   makes the next one free. When the card is full the till says so, and the
   free one comes off by itself. You do not press anything.
5. If they have a **promo code** (STUDENT10, say), type it in **Promo code**.
   The till shows what it takes off. If the code is stopped, unknown, or the
   order is too small for it, the till says why when you go to payment.
6. Tap **Go to payment**.

## Taking the money

Pick how they are paying:

- **Cash** — type what they handed you in *Cash given* and the change shows
  before you take the payment. Check it against the drawer.
- **Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, Zamtel Kwacha, Card** — take it on the operator's
  machine as usual, then type the **transaction number** it gives you. The
  system will not accept the payment without it, because that number is how the
  money is matched later. A number already used on another order is refused.
  Tick **I have seen the message saying the money arrived** only when you have
  actually seen it. Without the tick the payment is *pending* and a manager
  checks it against the statement later.
- **On account** — only for customers the manager has agreed to. Needs a name.

Someone can pay part in one way and the rest in another: take the first
payment, and the screen shows what is still owed.

When it is paid, tap **Print the receipt**, then **Next order**.

## Closing your shift

1. Count everything in the drawer, including the float.
2. Open **Till**, tap **Close the till**, and type the total you counted.
   The till does not show what it expects: count what is there.
3. If the drawer is out by more than the limit, the system asks **What
   happened?** Write it honestly and close again. Your first count is kept.
4. The system then shows what it expected and the difference.

A small difference happens. A manager will go through it with you. It is a
conversation, not an accusation, and nothing is taken from your pay.
You cannot close the till while an order on it is unpaid.

## When the connection drops

A yellow bar says "No connection to the server" and the till shows
**Offline**. Keep serving:

1. Take the order as usual. **Cash only**, no discounts, codes or stamp
   cards.
2. Press **Take cash (offline)**, type the cash given, press **Take cash**.
3. Print the receipt. It has a number like **T-3**. Give the kitchen its
   copy: their screen cannot see the order yet.
4. When the connection is back, the till sends the sales by itself. Leave
   the till signed in until the bar says nothing is waiting.

You cannot close the till while sales are waiting to send. If one shows
under "Offline sales the server could not take", call a manager; the cash
stays in the drawer.

## Things that go wrong

**"Open the till before taking an order."** You have not opened the till yet,
or someone else closed it.

**An item says sold out.** The kitchen marked it so. Offer something else.

**"That is more than the order owes."** Take the amount owing and give the
change from the drawer.

**You took the wrong order.** Before payment, tap **Clear**. After payment, ask
a manager — only they can refund or cancel.

**The screen says your session has ended.** Sign in again. Nothing is lost; the
order is still there.

**Never share your password.** If someone else needs to use the till, they sign
in themselves.

## Signing in

Tap **Cashier** under *I am signing in as*, then tap your name. Your email
fills in by itself; type your password and press **Sign in as Cashier**. If
the screen says "This is a … account", you picked the wrong card. Pick the one
it names.

## Your home screen

When you sign in you land on **My till**. It shows whether your till is
open and what the drawer should hold, what you have taken today, and the
orders that are ready for you to call out. If your account is on the staff
list, clock in and out from the top of this screen.

## Toppings

A bowl comes with five toppings. Tap more and the till adds K10 for each one
over five and shows it at the top of the toppings ("+K20.00 for 2 extra"), so
tell the customer before you add it to the order. Burritos take up to five;
more goes on as **Extra veggies**.

## Takeaway or eat in

Every order is one or the other. There are no deliveries: customers order and
collect at the counter.

## Clocking in

Tap **My clocking** in the sidebar, or use the button at the top of **My till**.
If it says your account is not on the staff list, ask the manager to add you.

## Printing a receipt

After payment, press **Print receipt**. Only the receipt prints. Choose the
till printer the first time; the browser remembers it.

## Cash to the safe, petty cash, cash added

On the till, tap **Cash drop or petty cash**, pick *Drop to the safe*,
*Petty cash spent* or *Cash added from the safe*, type the amount and why, and record it. Do this every
time cash leaves or enters the drawer other than a sale, or the till will look
short. A manager confirms it later.

## When a manager has to approve

A discount needs a reason. A big discount, or a refund, needs a manager. The
till shows a box: the manager types **their own** email and password there,
never yours, and you never type theirs. You cannot approve your own.

## Held orders and changing an order

An order not yet paid is listed under **Waiting for payment** on the till. **Take
payment** takes the money for it. **Change** puts it back in the cart; change
what you need and place it again: it keeps the same number. A paid order can't
be changed; ask a manager about a refund.

## Your profile and the lock screen

Tap your picture at the top right for your profile. There you can add a
photograph, your phone number and a new password, and see your own activity
and hours.

Leaving the counter for a moment? Tap your picture, then **Lock screen**. It
also locks by itself after 10 minutes untouched. Unlock it with your own
password; what you were doing is still there.

If someone else needs the till, they tap **Switch person** and sign in as
themselves. Your unfinished order waits for you on that computer. Never sell
under someone else's name.
