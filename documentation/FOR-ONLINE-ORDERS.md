# Online orders — what to do

Print this and put it where whoever watches the counter screen can see it.

## The screen

**Online** in the top bar. The first number on it is the one that matters:
**waiting for you**. If it is not zero, somebody is sitting somewhere with
their phone wondering whether we got their order.

The screen refreshes itself. You do not need to reload it.

## A new order comes in

1. Read it. Check we have everything on it and that the kitchen can cope.
2. **Accept** — the kitchen gets it straight away, and the customer is told.
3. If we cannot do it, **Cannot take it**, and say why in a few plain words.
   The customer is shown exactly what you write, so write it kindly:
   *"We have run out of chicken today"* and not *"unavailable"*.

Accept quickly even when the kitchen is busy. A customer who waits ten minutes
for an answer assumes we never saw it and rings.

## While it is being cooked

When the food is ready, press **Food is ready**.

- **Collection:** the customer is told to come. When they arrive, take the
  money at the till, then press **Collected**.
- **Delivery:** press **Send with a rider**, type who is taking it and their
  number, and it goes out. When they are back, press **Delivered**.

## Getting paid

Online orders are **not paid yet**. The money is taken at the till, exactly
like a counter sale:

- Collection: when they arrive.
- Delivery: when the rider comes back with the cash.

Use **Take payment at the till** on the order to go straight to it. Until that
is done, the sale is not in the books and the stock has not moved.

## Points

If the customer gave a phone number, their points are added automatically when
the order is paid for. You do not have to do anything.

At the counter, type a customer's phone number into the **Phone (for points)**
box on the till. If they have points, the till offers to use them. One hundred
points takes K5 off.

## Messages

The system writes every message it wants to send — *we have your order*, *it
is on the way* — but it cannot send them by itself yet. Open **Messages**,
press the WhatsApp button next to one, send it from the restaurant phone, and
press **I have sent it**.

Do this when things are quiet. The important ones are the refusals: somebody
whose order we turned down should hear so straight away.

## If something looks wrong

Nothing on this screen moves money except the till. If an order is wrong,
refuse it and ring the customer. Do not cancel an order that has already been
paid for — refund it through Orders instead, so the books follow.
